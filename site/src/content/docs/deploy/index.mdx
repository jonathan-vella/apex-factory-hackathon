Before day one, your team builds a simulated datacenter for each member and a shared Azure landing zone with GitHub Actions. Follow **Step 1 to Step 4** in order. Every stage runs from your own GitHub repository and leaves an auditable run log.

- **No coach is available during the pre-hackathon.** Pre-hackathon support is best-effort.
- **Your first point of contact is your designated team lead.** Check with your team lead first if you hit a permission, quota, or pipeline problem before day one.
- **Team leads:** your repo runs `member` and `teardown` only for the members you name.
- **A team is 3 to 5 people**, including the team lead. Every attendee is a **member** with their own repo and workload subscription. The **team lead** is a colleague the team nominates who also runs the shared foundation in Step 2 and vending in Step 4 (see [Roles](/getting-started/roles/)).

## Pre-hackathon steps in order

Complete the four steps below in chronological order before day one. Each page numbers its sub-steps (`1.1`, `1.2`, and so on); read the headings and surrounding instructions for who runs each command.

| Step | When | Who | What you do | Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Step 1** | T-14 (14 days before day one) | **Everyone** + **Team lead** | Create your private repo from `apex-accelerator`, import the kit, merge to `main`, create `.local/settings.json`, share roster values with your team lead, initialize the Actions pipeline, and run the `whatif` preflight and manual checks | [Step 1: Repo and preflight (T-14)](/deploy/set-up-your-repo/) |
| **Step 2** | T-7 (7 days before day one) | **Team lead** + **Privileged Role Administrator** | Deploy the team's shared landing zone (`foundation` stage) and grant the SQL Managed Instance identity its Microsoft Graph read permissions (`Grant-SqlMiDirectoryRead.ps1`). Members skip to Step 3 | [Step 2: Foundation and Graph grant (T-7)](/deploy/team-lead-steps/) |
| **Step 3** | T-3 (3 days before day one) | **Everyone** (team lead included) | Run the `member` stage (datacenter deploy, `Test-Datacenter.ps1` before Arc, and `vm-app01` Arc onboarding), start the first Arc SQL migration assessment, set up `vm-dev01` with Copilot, and commit `factory/evidence/readiness/` | [Step 3: Datacenter and workstation (T-3)](/deploy/member-steps/) |
| **Step 4** | T-1 (1 day before day one) | **Team lead** + **Everyone** | Everyone starts both datacenter VMs. The team lead runs the `vending` stage, which automatically checks that each member's datacenter and Arc connection are ready, connects each spoke to the hub, restarts the VMs, records exemptions, and runs connectivity probes | [Step 4: Vending (T-1)](/deploy/vending/) |
| **Event** | Day 1, 08:30 | **Everyone** | Attend the 30-minute tech lead introduction, then start [C1: Define the opportunity](/challenges/c01-define-the-opportunity/) at 09:00 | [Challenges](/challenges/) |
| **After** | T+2 | **Everyone**, **Team lead**, **Admin** | Tear down every member scope first, then the team scope, and remove the pipeline identities | [Tear down (T+2)](/deploy/tear-down/) |

## Automatic readiness check before vending

Your datacenter must be deployed, tested, and connected to Azure Arc in Step 3 before the team lead connects your subscription in Step 4. There is no coach sign-off gate before day one: when the team lead runs the `vending` stage in Step 4, the pipeline automatically verifies that each member's `vnet-datacenter` exists and `vm-app01` is Arc-connected (`Connected`) before vending their spoke. You also save `factory/evidence/readiness/` in Step 3 so your team lead (and your coach on day one) can inspect your setup if anything needs troubleshooting.
