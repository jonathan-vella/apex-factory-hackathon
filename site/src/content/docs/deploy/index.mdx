Before day one, your team builds a simulated datacenter and a landing zone (a ready-made, governed Azure foundation) with GitHub Actions. You set it up once, run each stage from your own repo and walk away. Every run leaves a log. This page shows who does what and when.

## Who is on the team

- A team is **at most 5 people** (3 to 5), including the team lead.
- The **team lead** is a colleague your team nominates. The lead does everything a member does, plus the team's foundation and the connection of every member.
- Each member has their own repo and their own workload subscription. The team shares one shared services subscription and one Microsoft Entra tenant.

The order is fixed. Follow it, not the order of the stages in the workflow.

## The readiness check

Your datacenter must work before anything else builds on it. The readiness check is a short list: the preflight is green, the datacenter test passes, Azure Arc is connected, and `vm-dev01` has Copilot working. A coach reviews your evidence and approves your readiness record. The team lead connects your subscription (vending) only after that approval, so a coach sign-off gates T-1. There are no points for it.
