This section introduces the challenges in order, with their time boxes and points. Each challenge page has the full brief: goal, scope, inputs, tasks, evidence, hints and bonus.

| Day | Challenge | Scope | Time box | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | [C1: Define the opportunity](./c01-define-the-opportunity/) | Team | 45 min | 10 |
| Day 1 | [C2: Secure, AI-ready foundation](./c02-secure-ai-ready-foundation/) | Team | 120 min | 35 |
| Day 1 | [C3: Assess the source](./c03-assess-the-source/) | Member | 60 min | 15 |
| Day 1 | [C4: Choose target states](./c04-choose-target-states/) | Team + member | 45 min | 25 |
| Day 1 | [C5: Deploy the CoE archetype with APEX](./c05-deploy-the-coe-archetype/) | Member | 90 min | 15 |
| Day 1–2 | [C6: Modernize with GHCP](./c06-modernize-with-ghcp/) | Member | 180 min | 30 |
| Day 2 | [C7: Migrate and go live](./c07-migrate-and-go-live/) | Member | 120 min | 20 |
| Day 2 | [C8: Validate the pattern](./c08-validate-the-pattern/) | Member | 45 min | 10 |
| Day 2 | [C9: Optimize the DB with GHCP](./c09-optimize-the-db-with-ghcp/) | Member | 60 min | 10 |
| Day 2 | [C10: Package, hand over, review AI readiness](./c10-package-hand-over-review-ai-readiness/) | Team | 60 min | 20 |

**Totals: 825 min across 10 challenges, 190 base points (80 team + 110 member).** Bonus points (noted per challenge, and from badges) are separate from this base contract. They share one cap of 30 points per team: the per-challenge maxima add up to more than that, and points beyond the cap aren't awarded. The maximum score is 220.

The readiness check is not a challenge. It is a gate before the event, with no points: your datacenter and `vm-dev01` must be ready, and a coach approves your readiness record, before the team lead vends your subscription. The steps are in [Deploy](../deploy/).

Time boxes are pacing, not hard stops. Coaches flex on the day. The event's real gates are T-14 (14 days before day one) and T-3 (3 days before day one), both before the event, and the end of each day. The [event overview](../getting-started/event-overview/#before-the-event) has both gates.

On a challenge page, tick **Done** on each task card as you finish it. The bar under the header shows how many tasks are done and jumps to each one. Ticks stay in this browser only: they aren't shared with your team, and clearing site data or switching browsers starts you from zero. **Reset** clears the page.

If you're blocked, try the hints on the page and the guide it links to, then ask your coach.
