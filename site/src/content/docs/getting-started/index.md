This section explains what you need, who does what and how the event runs. The steps to deploy are in [Deploy](../deploy/).

Start with [Event overview](./event-overview/), then read [Roles](./roles/) and [Requirements](./requirements/). Terms you don't know are in the [glossary](../reference/glossary/).

- [Event overview](./event-overview/): the timeline from T-14 to T+2, the gates and the event-day agenda.
- [Roles](./roles/): member, team lead, event owner, tech lead and coach.
- [Requirements](./requirements/): accounts, access, tools, network and team size.
- [The kit at a glance](./kit-at-a-glance/): where you run things, what changes, the ten challenges and the end state.
- [The customer story](./customer-story/): who Contoso University is, why it has to change and what its leaders want.
- [Learning before the event](../guides/learning-path/#stages): Stage 0 of the partner learning path. Learning only, no exams.

Then follow the deployment steps in order:

1. [Repo and preflight (T-14)](../deploy/set-up-your-repo/): everyone.
2. [Foundation and Graph grant (T-7)](../deploy/team-lead-steps/): team lead and Privileged Role Administrator.
3. [Datacenter and workstation (T-3)](../deploy/member-steps/): everyone.
4. [Vending (T-1)](../deploy/vending/): the team lead, after everyone starts their VMs.

After the event, follow [Tear down (T+2)](../deploy/tear-down/).
