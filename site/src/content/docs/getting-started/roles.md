## Platform lead

One per team. Holds the shared services subscription and the Tenant Root Owner rights needed to create management groups and move subscriptions. Runs ALZ-lite (C2), asks a Privileged Role Administrator to run the Graph grant, and vends every member, themselves included.

The platform lead is also a student. They hold their own workload subscription and a member index, do C0 like everyone else, and do every member challenge (C0, C3, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9) on it, as well as the team challenges (C1, C2, C4, C10). C4 has a team part and a member part.

## Members

Everyone else on the team: teams have three to five people, platform lead included. Each member has their own workload subscription and a member index (1 to 20), runs the member challenges independently, and joins the team challenges together with the rest of the team. One teammate creates the team repo in C1; your coach tells you who.

## Coach

Not a team member. Gives the live portal ALZ demo in C2 and the APEX archetype demo before C5, reviews your C0 output at T-3, signs off evidence and scores, runs the curveballs, assigns your member index (with the event owner), decides when and how to unblock a stuck member or team, and answers the kind of question a member genuinely can't resolve alone. A coach doesn't do the work for the team: ask your coach for help, not for the answer. Your coach is also your first contact at T-14 and T-3.

## Event owner

The partner practice lead who runs the event. Sets the dates, region and team size, builds the roster, names the platform leads, handles escalations that coaches pass up (such as a gate missed at T-14 or T-3), signs off the final scores and owns cleanup. If you can't reach your coach, contact the event owner through the escalation channel published with the agenda.
