title: Roles
description: Platform lead, members and coach — who does what.
---

## Platform lead

One per team. Holds the shared services subscription and the Tenant Root Owner rights needed to create management groups and move subscriptions. Runs ALZ-lite (C2) and vending for every member. Also participates in the team-scoped challenges (C1, C2, C4, C10) as a member.

## Members

Everyone else on the team. Each member has their own workload subscription, runs the member-scoped challenges (C0, C3, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9) independently, and joins the team-scoped challenges (C1, C2, C4, C10) together with the rest of the team.

## Coach

Not a team member. Runs the live portal ALZ demo in C2, reviews evidence, decides when and how to unblock a stuck member or team, and answers the kind of question a member genuinely can't resolve alone. A coach doesn't do the work for the team — ask your coach for help, not for the answer.
