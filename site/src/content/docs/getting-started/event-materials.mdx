Use these materials to introduce the event, prepare attendees and close the showcase. The presentations are editable PowerPoint files. Dates, time zones, venues and contacts remain placeholders so you can reuse the materials for each delivery.

## Download the presentations

- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Sell-Sheet.pptx" download>Partner leadership sell sheet</a> (2 slides, A4 portrait). Explain the benefits, what the team builds and keeps, and who should join.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Hackathon-and-What-You-Need.pptx" download>Orientation: the hackathon and what you need</a> (6 slides). Share the scenario, roles, access requirements and pre-event milestones with attendees.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Know-Before-You-Go.pptx" download>Know Before You Go: orientation and prework</a> (24 slides). Present the pre-event briefing and the T-14 to T-1 setup runbook.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Day1-Kickoff.pptx" download>Day 1 kickoff</a> (17 slides). Use for the tech lead's 30-minute opening slot at 08:30, followed by reference slides.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Closing-the-Event.pptx" download>Closing the event</a> (5 slides). Prepare during C10, then deliver the showcase in the 16:15 slot and confirm the teardown handoff.

Before each delivery, fill in the logistics in your own copies and reconfirm the current [requirements](../requirements/), [roles](../roles/) and [event overview](../event-overview/). The event owner supplies two or three full Azure Landing Zone comparison slides or a short recording for the Day 1 kickoff; that additional material is not included in the download.

Read the <a href="/downloads/description.txt" download>file descriptions</a> and <a href="/downloads/CHANGELOG.md" download>materials changelog</a> for the contents and verification details.
