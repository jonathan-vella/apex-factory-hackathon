Use these materials to introduce the event, prepare attendees and close the showcase. The presentations are editable PowerPoint files. Dates, time zones, venues and contacts remain placeholders so you can reuse the materials for each delivery.

## Download the presentations

Use the [requirements](../requirements/) and [deployment guide](/deploy/) for setup and teardown. They supersede the instructions in the slides.

- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Sell-Sheet.pptx" download>Partner leadership sell sheet</a> (2 slides, A4 portrait). Explain the benefits, what the team builds and keeps, and who should join.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Hackathon-and-What-You-Need.pptx" download>Hackathon orientation</a> (6 slides). Introduce the scenario, roles and two-day event.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Know-Before-You-Go.pptx" download>Know Before You Go</a> (24 slides). Use the scenario and platform slides for the pre-event briefing.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Day1-Kickoff.pptx" download>Day 1 kickoff</a> (17 slides). Use for the tech lead's 30-minute opening slot at 08:30, followed by reference slides.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Closing-the-Event.pptx" download>Closing the event</a> (5 slides). Prepare during C10, then deliver the showcase in the 16:15 slot and confirm the cleanup handoff.

Before each delivery, fill in the logistics in your own copies and review the current [roles](../roles/) and [event overview](../event-overview/). The event owner supplies two or three full Azure Landing Zone comparison slides or a short recording for the Day 1 kickoff; that additional material is not included in the download.

Read the <a href="/downloads/description.txt" download>file descriptions</a> and <a href="/downloads/CHANGELOG.md" download>materials changelog</a> for the contents and verification details.

## Prepare emails and invitations

Download the reusable text templates, or use the editable drafts below:

- <a href="/downloads/email%20about%20hackathon.txt" download>Overview email</a>.
- <a href="/downloads/invite%20-%20know%20before%20you%20go.txt" download>Know Before You Go invitation</a>.

Fill in the event details, choose **Path A (Self-service teams)** or **Path B (Lead-provisioned cohort)**, and adapt the text to your audience. The standard times follow the [two-day agenda](../event-overview/). Review and send the invitations yourself.
