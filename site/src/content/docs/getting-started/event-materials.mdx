Use these materials to brief attendees before the event, open Day 1, and run the Day 2 showcase and close. Both presentations are editable PowerPoint files. Dates, time zones, venues and contacts remain placeholders so you can reuse the materials for each delivery.

## Download the presentations

Use the [requirements](../requirements/) and [deployment guide](/deploy/) for setup and teardown. They are the authoritative reference for commands and prerequisites.

- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Know-Before-You-Go.pptx" download>Know Before You Go</a> (6 slides). Introduce the value proposition and Contoso University scenario, explain team roles and working locations, review the requirements checklist, compare **Path A (Self-service teams)** and **Path B (Lead-provisioned cohort)** across T-14 to T-1, and confirm Day 1 readiness and cost rules.
- <a href="/downloads/Partner-Modernization-Factory-Event-Deck.pptx" download>Event kickoff and close</a> (9 slides). Deliver the tech lead's 30-minute Day 1 opening slot at 08:30 (customer story, six outcomes, target architecture, Day 1 and Day 2 schedules, and launching C1 in slides 1–6) and the Day 2 close at 16:15 (C10 self-check, 5-minute showcase prompts, 200-point scoring recap, and T+2 teardown in slides 7–9).

Before each delivery, fill in the logistics in your own copies and review the current [roles](../roles/) and [event overview](../event-overview/). The event owner supplies two or three full Azure Landing Zone comparison slides or a short recording for the Day 1 kickoff; that additional material is not included in the download.

## Prepare emails and invitations

Fill in the event details below, choose **Path A (Self-service teams)** or **Path B (Lead-provisioned cohort)**, and adapt the generated overview email, Know Before You Go invitation, and two-day hackathon invitation to your audience. The standard times follow the [two-day agenda](../event-overview/). Review and send the invitations yourself.
